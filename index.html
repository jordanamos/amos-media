Amos Media request bot – privacy

This WhatsApp bot is a private service for Jordan Amos's family and friends to request movies and TV shows.

  • What it uses: your phone number and the messages you send it, only to answer you and file your requests in Jordan's media request system.
  • What it shares: your message text is sent to OpenAI to write the reply. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.
  • What it keeps: recent messages are held in memory for up to 24 hours and then forgotten. Requests stay in the request system, listed under your name.
  • Who can use it: only numbers Jordan has added. Anyone else is ignored.
  • Contact: ask Jordan to remove your number or your requests at any time.
